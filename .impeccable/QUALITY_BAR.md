# Quality bar

The approved build must read as an evaluation bench: the comparison matrix is the dominant mechanism and every gate resolves to inspectable evidence. Decorative score cards, hidden regressions, or chart-only claims fail the bar.

Reference challenger: [Broadcast Programming Teletext Service](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/broadcast-programming-teletext-service.webp) and its [hero](https://impeccable.style/worlds/cards/broadcast-programming-teletext-service-hero.webp). The commitment is information density and signal clarity, not copied styling.
