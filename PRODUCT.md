# EvalForge AI product brief

## Mode
Operate. The operator must recognize state, evidence, exceptions, and next safe action within seconds.

## User and task
An AI quality lead compares a release candidate with its baseline, selects a failed rubric, inspects the underlying evidence, and decides whether promotion should remain blocked.

## Non-negotiables
Synthetic data only; deterministic evaluator; no score without traceable cases; visible regression state; keyboard and mobile access; no model credential required.
